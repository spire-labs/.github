# Spire Labs

We are building the next generation of rollup technology, focusing on the development of the Based Stack to empower applications to seamlessly deploy their own appchains.

## About Us

Spire Labs is dedicated to advancing Ethereum scaling infrastructure. We aim to provide app developers with the tools they need to launch their own based rollup Layer 2 solutions, combining sovereignty and composability to overcome traditional appchain trade-offs.
